I am a PhD student at the [Speech Science group](https://www.coli.uni-saarland.de/groups/FK/speech_science/) at the department of Speech Science and Technology at Saarland University studying prosody acquisition in second language learners. I am part of the [Prosoflip project (French-German L2 Prosody: from the Lab into Practice)](https://www.uni-trier.de/universitaet/fachbereiche-faecher/fachbereich-ii/faecher/phonetik/forschung/prosoflip), which investigates French-German L2 prosody production and perception and evaluates different training methods for L2 prosody acquisition.

My research interests include: 

* Prosody
* Second language acquisition
* Gesture-speech interaction
* Speech variation
* Language teaching
